## Admetus's Competitive Advantages for These Markets

| Advantage | Why It Matters for Low-Regulation Markets |
|-----------|------------------------------------------|
| **Halal Certified** | Required in 30+ target countries (all Muslim nations). Most Indian softgel manufacturers are NOT Halal certified. |
| **WHO-GMP** | The minimum standard accepted across Africa, Middle East, SE Asia. Opens every market above. |
| **Low MOQ** | New distributors in Somalia, Yemen, Afghanistan can't commit to 1M+ capsules. Flexible MOQ is critical. |
| **Export Documentation** | COA, Free Sale Certificate, CPP — many small-market importers don't know what they need. Admetus can guide them. |
| **Product Range** | Vitamin D3 (malaria/deficiency markets), Omega-3 (cardiac markets), Multivitamins (general health) — all high-demand in Africa + Asia |
| **Price Competitiveness** | India is 40-60% cheaper than EU/US. For Afghanistan/Somalia/Yemen, price is THE decision factor. |
