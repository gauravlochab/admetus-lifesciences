## 2. Competitor Profiles

### #1 Apkavit Lifesciences Pvt. Ltd. — `apkavit.com`
- **Location:** Panchkula, **Haryana** (Sector 20) — *direct geo-competitor to Admetus*
- **Authority signals:** WHO-GMP, ISO claimed (no certificate badges shown). No Wikipedia presence. Domain age signals modest; corporate-style site.
- **Page structure:** Hero + multi-level nav (Services, Products, Export, Blog). Dedicated landing page per keyword variant (`/softgel-capsules-manufacturer-in-india/` is a hub).
- **Content depth:** ~900–1,100 words on the main softgel page, **9-question FAQ block**, blog, **no case studies**.
- **Schema markup:** Article + Organization + Person + WebPage + WebSite JSON-LD. **No Product, no FAQPage, no LocalBusiness** — gap.
- **Angle:** "Quality + scale + cost"; pushes low MOQs and budget-friendly manufacturing. Cites Grand View Research market data (E-E-A-T move).
- **Backlinks/press:** None visible on IndiaMart top-100, no press mentions detected.

### #2 Capsoft Healthcare — `capsoft.in`
- **Location:** Baddi, Himachal Pradesh
- **Authority signals:** Stacks 10 certifications (ISO 9001:2008, WHO, GLP, FDA, HACCP, MSME, FSSAI, GMP, cGMP, Make in India). Lists **40+ branded bioactives** (Carnipure, Quatrafolic, UC-II, Kaneka CoQ10, AstaReal, Setria, K2 Vital, Lycored) — strongest "ingredient credibility" signal of any competitor.
- **Page structure:** Deep navigation — Company / About Softgel / Expertise / Drug Approval / Branded Bioactive / Export / Blog / Careers. Page-per-segment (Omega-3, Bone Health, Eye Care, Cardiovascular, Hepatoprotective, Nephrology, Derma).
- **Content depth:** ~1,500–2,000 words on homepage. Blog stub linked. **No FAQ. No case studies.**
- **Schema markup:** Only WebSite + WebPage + BreadcrumbList + SearchAction. **No Organization, no Product, no FAQPage** — surprising gap given site depth.
- **Angle:** Scale-led — "1B capsules/year, 600+ drug formulations, 20+ export countries, 1500+ clients" hammered above the fold. Branded-ingredient specialist positioning.
- **Backlinks/press:** None observed on IndiaMart's top organic; no Wikipedia.

### #3 Medella Softgel Pvt. Ltd. — `medellasoftgel.com`
- **Location:** SIDCO Industrial Area, Kathua, **Jammu & Kashmir** (regional play, far from Haryana)
- **Authority signals:** WHO, GMP, GLP, ISO, MSME, Drug License — explicit list. Tagline: "WHO-GMP Certified Pharma Manufacturing Company in India."
- **Page structure:** **Programmatic state pages** — Maharashtra, Himachal Pradesh, Goa, Gujarat, Tamil Nadu, Andhra Pradesh, Karnataka, Pondicherry, Panchkula, Telangana. This is their #1 SEO weapon.
- **Content depth:** ~900–1,100 words homepage; 6-question FAQ; 10+ blog posts (active); 4 testimonials. **No case studies.**
- **Schema markup:** **Zero JSON-LD detected.** Major technical gap.
- **Angle:** Quality + cost-effectiveness; multi-state contract manufacturing.
- **Backlinks/press:** None observed.

### #4 United Laboratories — `unitedlabs.in`
- **Location:** Multi-unit (India ×2, Nepal, UK, Uganda) — strongest international footprint
- **Authority signals:** WHO, GMP, ISO 22000:2018, HALAL, KOSHER, HACCP, DCGI. **Google reviews 4.7 stars / 238 reviews** (the only competitor with social proof at scale). Claims "more than 15 awards" (India 5000 MSME, Pride of Bharat, Worldwide Achievers, etc.).
- **Page structure:** Hero + 20-subcategory product nav + Services + Awards + Certificates + Gallery + downloadable product PDFs (6+).
- **Content depth:** **~1,800–2,100 words** on main softgel page — deepest of competitor set. 6-question FAQ, blog format, video embed, Google reviews integrated. **No case studies.**
- **Schema markup:** Article + BreadcrumbList + ImageObject + Person + ListItem + ReadAction + SearchAction. **No Organization, no Product, no FAQPage** — but strong content schema.
- **Angle:** Quality + scale + global reach; "India's leading integrated manufacturer, supplier, and exporter, 15+ years."
- **Backlinks/press:** Awards links create natural backlinks; not seen on IndiaMart top organic; no Wikipedia.

### #5 Nukind Healthcare — `nukindhealthcare.com`  *(Haryana competitor — most relevant)*
- **Location:** **Faridabad, Haryana** (Sector 32, DLF Industrial Area) — direct Haryana rival
- **Authority signals:** WHO-GMP, ISO 9001:2015, FDA, HACCP. Tighter cert list than Capsoft/United, but explicit and consistent.

- **Schema markup:** Article + Organization + Person + WebPage + WebSite + ImageObject. Good baseline; no Product / FAQPage / LocalBusiness.
- **Angle:** Science-backed B2B preventive-healthcare framing; less price-led than the rest.
- **Backlinks/press:** None observed on IndiaMart top organic; no Wikipedia.

## 3. Pattern Analysis

### What every top-ranking competitor has that Admetus likely doesn't
1. **A keyword-targeted landing page** at a clean URL (`/softgel-capsule-manufacturer-in-india/`, `/nutraceutical-manufacturer-in-gurugram/`). Homepage rankings are rare.
2. **1,000–3,000 words** of body copy on that landing page (Nukind = 3k, United = 2k, Apkavit = 1k floor).
3. **A long FAQ block** (6–10 Qs covering MOQ, certifications, exports, customization) — every single competitor has this.
4. **Active blog** — even if thin, every site has /blog/.
5. **Cert list rendered as text** (so it's crawlable), not just a logo strip.
6. **Geo/state subpages** (Medella's 10-state model, Nukind's per-city pages) — a programmatic SEO play.

### What none of them have — Admetus's opportunity
- **`Product` and `FAQPage` JSON-LD schema** — zero of 7 competitors expose these. Adding them is cheap and could win rich snippets unilaterally.
- **`LocalBusiness` schema with proper NAP** — none have it. Critical for Haryana geo-queries.
- **Case studies** — zero of 7 have them. A real client story (anonymised batch result, stability data, formulation challenge solved) would be unique content.
- **Visible certificate PDFs** — only United Laboratories offers downloads. Hosting Admetus's FSSAI/GMP/HACCP/Halal/WHO-GMP certs as PDFs creates internal link targets and trust.
- **Equipment specificity** — nobody names their encapsulation line. Admetus naming "ARBES SGX-806P" is a unique, citable, GEO-friendly fact.

### Common angle distribution
- **Price-led:** Apkavit, Medella, Planet India
- **Scale-led:** Capsoft, United Laboratories
- **Quality/specialty-led:** Nukind, Growequal, **opening for Admetus**
- **Ingredient-specialist:** Capsoft (only one)

## 4. Backlink / press environment

- **None of the top 7 ranked sites appear in the IndiaMart top organic** for "softgel capsule manufacturer" (those slots are occupied by Yaxon Biocare, Jaiwik Biotech, Knox Life Sciences, Novalab, Elikem — different ecosystem). This suggests competitors won via **on-page SEO, not directory backlinks**.
- **No Wikipedia entries** for any of the 7 brands.
- **No EIN Newswire / PR Newswire press hits** discoverable for these brands.
- United Laboratories' Awards page is the only organic backlink magnet (each award association links back).
- **Implication:** Admetus is competing in a low-DA space (estimated DA 10–25 across competitors). Wins are achievable on content quality + schema, not link volume.

## 5. Beatability Assessment

### Realistic 3–6 month wins (long-tail)

**Tier A — high probability of cracking Top 10**
1. **"softgel manufacturer Haryana" / "softgel manufacturer Sonipat" / "softgel contract manufacturer Sonipat / Panchkula / Faridabad"** — Geo-modified long-tails where the only serious Haryana competitor is **Nukind (Faridabad)** and **Apkavit (Panchkula)**. Admetus can build a `/softgel-manufacturer-haryana/` page with LocalBusiness schema, 1,500+ words, FAQ block, and outrank Nukind's generic Gurugram page within 3–4 months. Apkavit is more entrenched but beatable on long-tail.
2. **"precision softgel manufacturer India" / "small batch softgel manufacturer India" / "ARBES softgel manufacturer"** — These exact phrases have near-zero competition. Admetus's equipment specificity is a moat. Easy Top 5 in 60–90 days.
3. **"FSSAI Halal softgel manufacturer India"** — Only United Labs explicitly stacks Halal+Kosher; nobody owns the FSSAI+Halal combo as a phrase. Buildable.

**Tier B — beatable with effort (4–6 months)**
4. **Apkavit Lifesciences (Panchkula)** — closest Haryana rival. Their content is thin (~1k words) and schema is incomplete. Admetus can outproduce on word count, FAQ depth, case studies, and Product/FAQPage schema. Realistic to displace on "softgel manufacturer Panchkula" and "third party softgel Haryana" long-tails.
5. **Medella Softgel (J&K)** — geographically distant, but their state-page programmatic play is replicable. Zero schema means they're vulnerable to a technically clean Admetus geo-page sweep.

### Out of reach without serious link-building budget (12+ months)

6. **Capsoft Healthcare** — 40+ branded bioactives + Baddi pharma cluster + 10-cert stack. To match their "ingredient credibility" requires 6–12 months of content + supplier partnerships. Their on-page is also denser. Don't fight them head-on for "softgel capsule manufacturer India" generic.
7. **United Laboratories** — multi-country footprint, 238 Google reviews, 15+ awards, 15+ years brand presence. Their authority compounds. Outranking them on any non-geo-qualified head term in <12 months would need budget for digital PR (HARO, niche pharma trade publications, EIN Newswire releases). Skip for now.
8. **Lifevision Healthcare** — owns "Top 10..." listicle SERP feature; aggregator strategy is hard to displace without writing an even better listicle (and convincing Google it's authoritative).

### Recommended attack plan for Admetus (next 90 days)

1. **Build 5 keyword-targeted landing pages**: `/softgel-capsule-manufacturer-india/`, `/softgel-contract-manufacturer-haryana/`, `/private-label-softgel-manufacturer-india/`, `/third-party-softgel-manufacturer-sonipat/`, `/nutraceutical-contract-manufacturer-haryana/`. Each ≥1,800 words, with 8+ FAQ items.
2. **Add Product schema** for each of the 10 formulations + **FAQPage schema** + **LocalBusiness schema** with full Haryana NAP. (Admetus already has Organization + PostalAddress — extend, don't restart.)
3. **Publish 2 case studies** — even anonymised ("Client X: omega-3 stability problem solved in 4 weeks"). Zero competitors have these.
4. **Host certificate PDFs** at `/certifications/fssai.pdf` etc., link from every landing page. Mirrors United Labs's only real moat.
5. **Lean into "ARBES SGX-806P precision encapsulation"** as a brand differentiator phrase — own that exact-match SERP completely.
6. **List on IndiaMart and TradeIndia** anyway — even though top competitors didn't win via these directories, the directory profiles add NAP citations and rank for unbranded "near me" queries.
