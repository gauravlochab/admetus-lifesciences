## How to read this table

- **Domain Rating (DR)** estimates are based on publicly known referring-domain counts and traffic data for these well-known platforms; verify with Ahrefs/Moz before any paid commitment.
- **DoFollow vs NoFollow** is verified by inspecting `rel=` on outbound profile/website links from existing member profiles where accessible. Where unverifiable from the public pages, it is marked **"Likely [X] (verify)"**.
- **Effort tiers:** Light (≤30 min profile), Medium (1–3 hrs catalog upload), Heavy (multi-day docs / vetting).
- **Lead-gen rating:** ★★★★★ scale based on platform purpose: marketplaces ≥ ★★★★, association directories ★★, regulatory ★ (SEO-only), press ★★ (brand discovery).
