# Design Context — Admetus Lifesciences

## Target Audience
- International pharmaceutical buyers, importers, distributors
- Private label supplement brand owners
- Contract manufacturing clients seeking softgel capsule production
- B2B procurement directors at nutraceutical companies
- Export partners and regulatory professionals

## Use Cases
- Evaluate Admetus as a manufacturing partner (credibility assessment)
- Browse product portfolio (7 softgel capsule lines)
- Submit bulk/export enquiry or request a quote
- Assess manufacturing capability and quality certifications
- Explore contract manufacturing and private label options

## Brand Personality / Tone
- **Cinematic** — the site should feel like a premium product launch, not a corporate brochure
- **Science-forward** — precision, molecular, clinical confidence
- **Globally credible** — speaks to international buyers with authority
- **Minimal but art-directed** — generous whitespace, editorial layout, strong visual hierarchy
- **Trustworthy** — certifications, facility photos, quality metrics build confidence
- **Modern pharmaceutical** — clean, warm dark aesthetic, not cold clinical blue

## Visual Direction
- Inspired by oryzo.ai's design language: scroll-driven storytelling, oversized typography, full-screen immersive sections
- Dark warm palette: #0A0A0A (black), #141414 (charcoal), #1A1710 (warm dark), #F5F0E8 (cream)
- Gold accent (#C8A951) for CTAs and highlights, teal (#0097A7) for secondary
- Display font: Plus Jakarta Sans (800 weight, ALL-CAPS, -0.03em tracking)
- Body: Inter, Mono: DM Mono
- GSAP ScrollTrigger for pinned reveals, horizontal scroll, parallax
- Lenis smooth scrolling
- Sharp-edged buttons (no border-radius) for editorial feel
- Real pharmaceutical imagery (Unsplash placeholders for now)

## Tech Stack
- Next.js 16 (App Router, React 19, TypeScript)
- Tailwind CSS v4
- shadcn/ui
- GSAP + ScrollTrigger
- Lenis smooth scroll
- Motion (Framer Motion) for page transitions
- Static export for Cloudflare Pages

## Pages
- Homepage (12 cinematic sections)
- About, Products (index + 7 detail), Manufacturing, Quality, Contract Manufacturing, Export, Contact

## What to avoid
- Generic "AI slop" — Inter-everywhere, purple gradients, nested cards, rounded corners everywhere
- Corporate pharma cliches — "committed to excellence", stock photos of handshakes
- Cluttered layouts — IndiaMART style
- Consumer supplement store look
- Bouncy/elastic animations
