## PHONE CALL SCRIPTS

### Script 1: Cold Call to Founder/CEO
*Best for: Small pharma company founders found via IndiaMART, JustDial, or LinkedIn*

**Prep before calling:**
- Know their company name, products, and city
- Have their website open on your screen
- Best calling window: 10:00-11:30 AM or 2:00-3:30 PM IST (Tue-Thu)

```
[Phone rings, they pick up]

You: "Hi, am I speaking with {FirstName}?"

Them: "Yes, who's this?"

You: "Hi {FirstName}, this is {YourName} from Admetus Life Sciences. We're a 
softgel capsule manufacturing company. I'll keep this very brief — is this a 
good time for a 2-minute conversation?"

[If no]: "No problem at all. When would be a better time to call? I'll call 
back then." [Note the time, call back exactly when promised]

[If yes]:

You: "Thank you. I came across {CompanyName} — looks like you're doing great 
work in the {nutraceutical/pharma} space in {City}.

Quick question: are you currently manufacturing your softgel products in-house, 
or are you working with a contract manufacturer?"

[If they DON'T have softgels]: 
You: "Got it. The reason I ask is that softgel capsules are the fastest-growing 
format in nutraceuticals right now — better absorption, premium look, higher 
margins. If {CompanyName} is thinking about adding softgels to your product 
line, we can get you from formulation to first batch in about 6 weeks. We're 
WHO-GMP certified with 40+ formulations ready to go. Would it be worth 
exploring?"

[If they have a CMO already]: 
You: "That's great. Are you fully satisfied with your current manufacturing 
partner, or is there room for a secondary supplier? Many companies we work with 
use us as a backup or for specific formulations. We specialize exclusively in 
softgels, which means faster turnaround and more focused quality control."

[If interested]:
You: "Excellent. What would be the best next step — should I send you our 
product catalog and certifications on WhatsApp or email? And would a 15-minute 
call later this week work to discuss your specific requirements in detail?"

[Close]:
You: "Perfect, I'll send that over right away. Thanks for your time, 
{FirstName}. Looking forward to working together."
```

### Script 2: Cold Call to Procurement/BD Head
*Best for: Mid-size company contacts found via LinkedIn or IDMA directory*

```
You: "Hi {FirstName}, this is {YourName} from Admetus Life Sciences. We're a 
WHO-GMP certified softgel capsule manufacturer. I'm reaching out because I 
believe we could be a good manufacturing partner for {CompanyName}. Do you have 
a minute?"

[If yes]:

You: "We specialize exclusively in nutraceutical softgel encapsulation — 
Omega-3, Calcium formulations, Multivitamins, Antioxidants, and more. We have 
40+ validated formulations with full documentation.

I wanted to check: is {CompanyName} looking to either expand your softgel 
product line or evaluate additional manufacturing partners this year?"

[If they have a formal vendor process]:
You: "Completely understand. Would it help if I sent across our capability 
deck and certifications so your team can review? We also welcome facility 
audits — many of our clients visit our plant before finalizing."

[If interested in learning more]:
You: "Great. The quickest way to move forward would be:
1. I'll send you our product catalog with all formulations and pricing ranges
2. We can schedule a 20-minute call with your quality team if needed
3. If it looks like a fit, we can discuss a pilot batch

What works best — email or WhatsApp for the catalog?"
```

### Script 4: Inbound Call Handler (From IndiaMART/Website Enquiry)
```
You: "Hi, this is {YourName} from Admetus Life Sciences. Thank you for 
reaching out! I understand you're looking for {what they enquired about}. 
Let me ask a few quick questions so I can give you the most relevant 
information:

1. Which formulations are you interested in? 
   (Omega-3, Calcium, Multivitamin, or something custom?)

2. What's your expected order quantity per batch?

3. Do you need private labeling — your own brand on the packaging?

4. Any specific packaging requirements? 
   (Blister packs, bottles, custom cartons?)

5. What's your target timeline for the first batch?

[After getting answers]:

You: "Perfect, this is exactly what we do. Based on what you've told me, 
I can send you:
- Our product catalog with the relevant formulations
- Pricing for your batch size
- Our certifications (WHO-GMP, FSSAI, HACCP, Halal)
- Sample packaging options

I'll send this on WhatsApp/email within the next 2 hours. And I'd suggest 
we schedule a follow-up call for {day} to go through everything in detail. 
Does that work?"
```

## OBJECTION HANDLING CHEAT SHEET

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "We already have a manufacturer" | "That's great. Many of our clients use us as a secondary supplier for specific formulations or during peak demand. Would it make sense to have a backup option?" |
| "Your facility is in Haryana, too far" | "We handle all logistics — door-to-door delivery anywhere in India. Many of our South India clients haven't visited the plant and still work with us based on our documentation and quality. We also welcome video facility tours." |
| "Send me the pricing on WhatsApp" | "Absolutely, sending it right now. Our pricing varies by formulation and batch size — once you review, a 10-minute call would help me give you the most accurate quote." |
| "We'll think about it" | "Of course, take your time. Would it help if I shared a sample with relevant certifications so your quality team can evaluate? No commitment needed." |
| "Your MOQ is too high" | "We're quite flexible on MOQs, especially for first-time orders. Let me check what the minimum would be for {their formulation} — I'll get back to you by tomorrow." |
| "We're not looking right now" | "Understood. When do you typically plan your product launches for the next year? I'd love to reconnect at the right time. Is Q3/Q4 better?" |
| "How do we know the quality is good?" | "Great question. We're WHO-GMP, FSSAI, and HACCP certified. We do 100% batch testing and provide full documentation — COA, batch records, stability data. We also welcome your team for a facility audit anytime." |
| "Can you do custom formulations?" | "Yes, we do custom formulation development. If you share the API/ingredient specification, we can work on a formulation and provide a timeline and costing." |
