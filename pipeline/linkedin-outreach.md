1. Send 10-15 personalized connection requests
2. Engage with 5-10 posts from target prospects (like + comment)
3. Post 1 piece of content (3-4x per week)
4. Follow up with accepted connections (1-2 days after acceptance)

*Goal: Build credibility and trust through transparency*

**Post ideas:**
- Video/photo of the ARBES encapsulation machine running a batch
- "A day at our softgel facility" walkthrough
- Time-lapse of a batch going from raw materials to finished packaging
- "What does 100% batch testing actually look like?" with photos of QC lab
- Before/after: raw gelatin preparation to finished softgel capsule

**Example post:**
```
Most people don't know what goes into making a single softgel capsule.

At Admetus Life Sciences, every batch goes through 7 stages:
1. Raw material sourcing from certified suppliers
2. Identity and purity testing
3. Gelatin preparation and formulation
4. Encapsulation on our ARBES SGX-806P line
5. Controlled drying and tumbling
6. 100% visual + automated inspection
7. Tamper-evident blister packaging

The result? Capsules that meet WHO-GMP, FSSAI, and HACCP standards — every single time.

If you're a brand owner looking for a manufacturing partner who takes quality this seriously, let's talk.
```
