## Timing & Sending Rules

1. **Best send times (India):** Tuesday-Thursday, 10:00-11:30 AM IST or 2:00-3:30 PM IST
2. **Avoid:** Monday mornings (inbox overload), Friday afternoons (checked out), weekends
3. **Response window:** If they reply, respond within 2 hours during business hours
4. **Max 1 sequence per company** — don't email the founder AND the procurement head simultaneously
5. **Stop the sequence immediately** if they reply (positive OR negative)
6. **Wait 90 days** before re-entering a non-responsive lead into a new sequence
