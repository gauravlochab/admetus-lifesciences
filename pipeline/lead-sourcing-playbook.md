# Admetus Life Sciences — Lead Sourcing Playbook

## Goal: 50-60 Qualified Leads in South Bangalore, Mumbai & South India
**Target:** Small-to-mid pharma/nutraceutical companies WITHOUT in-house manufacturing
**Exclude:** Large corporates (Sun Pharma, Cipla, Dr. Reddy's, Lupin, etc.)
**Decision Maker:** Founder/CEO (small companies), BD Head or Procurement Head (mid-size)

---

## SOURCE 1: IndiaMART (Expected: 15-20 leads)

### What It Is
India's largest B2B marketplace. Small pharma companies actively search here for contract manufacturers — AND list themselves as pharma product sellers.

### Step-by-Step

1. **Go to:** https://www.indiamart.com
2. **Search these terms** (one by one, filter by city):
   - `nutraceutical company`
   - `pharma company`
   - `food supplement company`
   - `health supplement brand`
   - `softgel capsules`
   - `pharmaceutical products`
   - `dietary supplement manufacturer`

3. **Apply location filters:**
   - Bangalore (Karnataka)
   - Mumbai (Maharashtra)
   - Chennai (Tamil Nadu)
   - Hyderabad (Telangana)
   - Pune (Maharashtra)
   - Kochi (Kerala)
   - Coimbatore (Tamil Nadu)

4. **For each company that appears:**
   - Check if they're a BRAND/MARKETER (not a manufacturer) — these are your prospects
   - Look for: "We are engaged in offering..." / "We are a leading trader/supplier of..."
   - Companies that TRADE or SUPPLY but don't MANUFACTURE are your ideal targets
   - Note the contact person name, phone number, and city
   - Add to `lead-tracker.csv`

5. **Also check buyer enquiries:**
   - If you create a seller account, you'll see active RFQs (Request for Quotes) from people searching for contract manufacturing
   - These are hot inbound leads — respond within 1 hour

### Pro Tips
- Companies with a "Verified" badge are more legitimate
- Check their "Nature of Business" field — look for "Trader", "Wholesaler", "Exporter" (not "Manufacturer")
- Companies listing softgel products but showing no factory photos likely outsource manufacturing

## SOURCE 2: PharmaHopers (Expected: 10-15 leads)

### What It Is
India's dedicated pharma B2B directory. Companies list themselves by category and city.

### Step-by-Step

1. **Go to:** https://www.pharmahopers.com
2. **Navigate to:** Pharma Companies section
3. **Search by categories:**
   - "Nutraceutical Companies"
   - "Third Party Manufacturing"
   - "Pharma PCD Franchise Companies" (these companies NEED manufacturers)
   - "Pharma Marketing Companies" (they outsource manufacturing)

4. **Filter by city:** Bangalore, Mumbai, Chennai, Hyderabad

5. **For PCD Franchise companies specifically:**
   - These are companies that do "Propaganda Cum Distribution" — they market pharma products under their brand but DON'T manufacture
   - They are PERFECT prospects for contract manufacturing
   - Search: https://www.pharmahopers.com/pharma/pcd-pharma-franchise

6. **Extract:** Company name, contact person, phone, email, city, product focus

## SOURCE 3: CDSCO & Government Drug Databases (Expected: 10-15 leads)

### What It Is
Government databases of licensed pharma companies. Gold mine for finding legitimate, licensed companies that may need manufacturing partners.

### Step-by-Step

**A. CDSCO Data Bank**
1. **Go to:** https://cdsco.gov.in/opencms/opencms/en/Data-Bank/
2. **Look for:**
   - "List of outlets having drug licences" (filtered by state)
   - "New Drug Approvals" — companies that just got new drugs approved NEED manufacturing capacity
   - "WHO-GMP Certified Units" — these are manufacturers (your competitors, not prospects)
3. **Filter for:** Companies that hold MARKETING licenses but NOT manufacturing licenses — they need someone like you to manufacture

**B. ONDLS (One Nation One Drug Licensing System)**
1. **Go to:** https://statedrugs.gov.in
2. **Search by state:** Karnataka, Maharashtra, Tamil Nadu, Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, Kerala
3. **Look for:** Drug license holders with Form 20B/21B (wholesale/retail) rather than Form 25/28 (manufacturing)
4. **These companies** have licenses to sell/distribute but not manufacture — ideal prospects

**C. Karnataka Drugs Control**
1. **Go to:** https://drugs.kar.nic.in
2. **Navigate to:** Drug licence holders list
3. **Look for:** Companies in Bangalore with marketing/trading licenses

**D. Maharashtra FDA**
1. **Go to:** https://fdawhogmp.maharashtra.gov.in
2. **Use:** "Manufacturer Profile Search" — but REVERSE IT
3. **Search companies** that are NOT in the manufacturer list — they'll need contract manufacturing

## SOURCE 5: IDMA Member Directory (Expected: 5-10 leads)

### What It Is
Indian Drug Manufacturers' Association — 1,000+ member companies. Despite the name, many members are marketers/traders, not manufacturers.

### Step-by-Step

1. **Go to:** https://idma-assn.org/member/view
2. **Filter by state boards:**
   - Karnataka State Board
   - Tamil Nadu State Board
   - Maharashtra (multiple offices)
3. **Download/browse** the member list
4. **Cross-reference** with manufacturing license databases — members WITHOUT manufacturing licenses are your prospects
5. **Also check Scribd** for downloadable member lists: search "IDMA members directory" on scribd.com

## LEAD QUALIFICATION CHECKLIST

Before adding to your final lead list, verify:

- [ ] Company is in pharma/nutraceutical space (not biotech, medtech, or animal health)
- [ ] Company is in target geography (South Bangalore, Mumbai, South India)
- [ ] Company does NOT have its own manufacturing facility (they're a marketer/trader/brand owner)
- [ ] Company appears to have <200 employees (not a large corporate)
- [ ] You have at least a phone number OR email for a decision-maker
- [ ] Company has active products in the market (not dormant/shell company)

**Lead Score:**
| Score | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 5 | Perfect fit: small company, no manufacturing, in target city, have direct contact for founder/CEO |
| 4 | Good fit: meets most criteria, may need to find better contact |
| 3 | Decent fit: right industry but may be slightly outside target (e.g., slightly larger company) |
| 2 | Marginal: right industry but limited info or unclear manufacturing status |
| 1 | Long shot: may not need contract manufacturing, or info is thin |
